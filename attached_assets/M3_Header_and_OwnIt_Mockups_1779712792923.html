What lives in you
Three creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch.
The seer in me
In my power, I am the Cleverly Wise Owl.
The Owl sees what others miss, gives me steady discernment, and gives others permission to slow down.
Under pressure, I judge, costing me openness, and costing others opportunities with others.
Both are mine.
The anchor in me
In my power, I am the Gracefully Strong Bear.
The Bear holds steady when others can't, gives me unshakable ground, and gives others a place to come home to.
Under pressure, I hide, costing me opportunities, and costing others lack of seeing me.
Both are mine.

And when I go shadow, I judge and I hide.
Both are mine. Owning both is how I come home.
