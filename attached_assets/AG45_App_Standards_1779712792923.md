# AG45 True Ownership · App Standards (v1.1)

**What this is:** The single source of truth for app-wide patterns, constants, and conventions across the True Ownership app (Boardroom + Owner's Circle). Read this before every ship. Update it when patterns change.

**Why:** Without a standards doc, every ship is a guess at what the inherited pattern is. This eliminates drift.

**Rule for Replit Agent:** Standards in this doc are *defaults* — only override with explicit owner approval, and update this doc when approved overrides become the new standard.

---

## 1 · Veil Transition (room entry)

**Single source of truth:** `NA1_VEIL_TIMING` constant in `App.tsx`. Used by M1 (Purpose), M2 (Vision · 5 rooms), M3 (Identity · 4 rooms). Future mirrors inherit.

### Current locked values

```typescript
NA1_VEIL_TIMING = {
  holdAt: 1100,      // ms — peak hold, gold text held at center
  fallingAt: 1800,   // ms — veil begins peel from top
  idleAt: 2800       // ms — total cycle complete, room interactive
}
// Total: ~2.2 seconds
```

### Visual stages

| Stage | Duration | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Rise | 0–600ms | Radial gold-tinted veil sweeps up from bottom |
| Text fade-in | 400–900ms (overlaps) | Italic Georgia gold serif line rises + fades in |
| **HOLD** | 900–1800ms | User reads threshold line · screen swap happens invisibly underneath |
| Text fade-out | 1500–1800ms | Threshold text fades |
| Fall | 1800–2500ms | Veil peels back from top |
| Settle | 2500–2800ms | Room fully interactive |

### Veil text per room (italic Georgia gold serif)

**Mirror 1 — Purpose**
- Room entry → *"Stepping into the room with Katherine…"*

**Mirror 2 — Vision**
- Two Futures → *"Looking out ten years…"*
- Time · 10-Year → *"Stepping into the life you'll own…"*
- Wealth · 10-Year → *"Stepping into what you'll build…"*
- Self · 10-Year → *"Stepping into who you'll be…"*
- Vision Declaration → *"The moment of claiming…"*

**Mirror 3 — Identity**
- Your Gifts → *"Looking at what you carry…"*
- Superpowers → *"Giving them a shape…"* *(renamed Ship 5.5a — internal M3RoomKey `"animals"` preserved for state stability; only the user-visible room name changed)*
- Under Pressure → *"Where the gifts go shadow…"*
- Own It → *"The moment of claiming…"*

**A2 · Align With It**

**Mirror 2 — Time Audit**
- Room entry → *"Looking at where your time actually goes…"*

**Mirror 3 — Named Truth**
- The gap (room entry) → *"Looking at what the truth is showing you…"*
- Rethink your time (step entry) → *"This is where it shifts…"*
- Claim (step entry) → *"The moment of naming it…"*

### Rules

- **Veil fires only on room ENTRY, never on internal screen swaps.** Two Futures has 8 internal screens but only 1 veil (on entry).
- **Veil fires only for rooms in "Up next" or "In progress" state.** Locked rooms are tap-inert — no veil, no overlay, no transition.
- **CSS class `.na1-veil` is the single shared component.** Do not fork per-mirror. Future timing changes propagate from one constant.

## 2 · Continue Button Pattern

**Standardized across the entire app.** Sentence case, with arrow, naming the next destination.

### Format

`Continue to [Next Room Name] →`

### Examples (locked)

**Mirror 2 — Vision (internal room-to-room):**
- End of Two Futures → `Continue to Time →`
- End of Time → `Continue to Wealth →`
- End of Wealth → `Continue to Self →`
- End of Self → `Continue to Vision Declaration →`
- End of Vision Declaration (first claim) → `Continue to Identity →`

**Mirror 3 — Identity (internal room-to-room):**
- End of Your Gifts → `Continue to Superpowers →` *(renamed Ship 5.5a from `Continue to Animals →`)*
- End of Superpowers → `Continue to Under Pressure →`
- End of Under Pressure → `Continue to Own It →`
- End of Own It → `Continue to Values →` (lands when M4 ships)

### Exceptions

- **Inter-screen continues within a single exercise** stay generic `Continue →`. Two Futures internal screens (e.g. screens 3 and 7 of the chip-ladder flow) use plain `Continue →` because they advance to the next *screen of the same exercise*, not a new room.
- **Test for which to use:** if the destination is a new named room on the hub, use `Continue to [Name] →`. If the destination is the next internal screen of the current exercise, use `Continue →`.

### Styling

- Sentence case throughout — capital C on "Continue," capital first letter of each word in the room name
- Trailing arrow → (single character, not the unicode block arrow)
- Disabled at 50% opacity until validation passes
- Right-aligned in the room footer

## 4 · Typography

### Fonts

- **Body:** Inter (or system fallback)
- **Serif headings · editorial italic:** Cormorant Garamond (or Georgia fallback for body italic prose)
- **Veil text:** italic Georgia (NOT Cormorant — Georgia is the system fallback that renders consistently across devices)

### Type scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Tracking | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Eyebrow** (room label) | 10–11px | 700 | 0.18–0.22em | Gold uppercase |
| **H1** (room title) | Serif, ~32px | Regular | — | Default text |
| **Subhead** (italic gold serif) | ~18px | Italic | — | Gold |
| **Help card text** | ~15px | Italic | — | Gold inside burgundy border (Under Pressure) or gold border (other rooms) |
| **Body prose** | 16px | Regular | — | Default |
| **Filled blanks** (sentence completion) | Inherits prose size | Italic | — | Lens color |

### Eyebrow format

```
MIRROR [N] OF 4 · STEP [N] · [ROOM TITLE]
```

Examples:
- `MIRROR 3 OF 4 · STEP 1 · SHAPE YOUR VERSIONS`
- `MIRROR 3 OF 4 · STEP 3 · UNDER PRESSURE`

### Gift Inventory bar (Superpowers room · Ship 5.5a)

Sticky at top of the Superpowers room body — under the room header, above the first cluster card. Single source of truth for which gifts are picked + where each one lives.

**Structure:**
- Sticky position, `var(--bg-card)` background, `var(--gold-border)` border
- **Header row:** gold uppercase tracked label `YOUR GIFTS · N PICKED` (left) · count summary (right): `"all placed in creatures"` or `"N in creatures · M to place"`
- **Body:** 5 theme rows (Mind / Heart / Spine / Spark / Hands) — theme color label on left, chip stack on right
- **Legend:** italic gold serif at bottom — *"Tap any gift to move it to a different creature · + add on any theme to add a new gift (you'll pick which creature carries it)"*

**Theme colors:** Mind `#6A9BD8` · Heart `#D87A7A` · Spine `#C9A24B` · Spark `#C285D8` · Hands `#7AC79B`

**Chip states:**
| State | Treatment |
|---|---|
| In-cluster (Owned) | Gold-filled chip, dark navy text |
| In-cluster (Emerging) | Transparent · gold border · gold text · ✦ marker |
| Unplaced | Transparent · theme-color border · theme-color text · theme-tinted bg at low opacity |

**"+ add" affordance** sits at the end of every theme row (dashed gold border). On click → opens the Step 1a gift library filtered to that theme, respecting the per-theme 5-cap. Picked gift → triggers the "Which creature?" picker.

### "Which creature?" picker (popover · Ship 5.5a)

Small floating panel that anchors near the tapped chip. Used in three flows: (1) placing a newly added gift, (2) moving a gift from the inventory bar, (3) moving a gift from inside a cluster card.

**Visual:**
- ~380px wide · `rgba(15, 26, 48, 0.95)` bg · 1px gold border · 8px radius
- **Header:** gold uppercase tracked label, then italic serif sentence
  - Add: *"Which creature carries {giftName}?"*
  - Move: *"Move {giftName} to which creature?"*
- **Body:** vertical stack of creature buttons (one per existing cluster) — emoji + full descriptor (e.g. *"🦅 Gracefully Wise Eagle"*). Current creature appears grayed/disabled on move actions.
- **Footer:** italic helper *"Tap the creature that carries this gift in its power"*

**Behavior:**
- Tap a creature → picker closes, gift moves, inventory + cluster cards update instantly
- Tap outside / Escape → picker closes silently (move action only — add actions require placement)

## 6 · Sentence Completion Grammar

### Pattern

`"[Stem opening], [blank 1], and [blank 2]."` (M2 — two blanks · "behavior + proof")
`"[Stem opening], [blank 1], [blank 2], and [blank 3]."` (M3 Under Pressure — three blanks · "what I do + what it costs me + what it costs others")

### Rules

- Each blank is a **full-width line-break input below the corresponding sentence half** — NOT inline (except Under Pressure where the three blanks are inline)
- Placeholder: italic muted serif, lowercase
- Gold-tinted background until filled
- Once filled, user's words render in **lens color italic serif** (lens color depends on the room)
- Auto-saves on blur or Cmd+Enter
- **Validation:** all blanks must be filled to mark the exercise complete

### M2 Templates (locked)

| Domain | Lens | Sentence template |
|---|---|---|
| Time · Work | Yellow | "In 10 years, {chip}. I do this by ___, and the work itself feels like ___." |
| Time · Family | Yellow | "In 10 years, {chip}. I make this real by ___, and what I notice in our time together is ___." |
| Wealth · Cashflow | Green | "In 10 years, {chip}. I keep it healthy by ___, and what it lets me say yes to is ___." |
| Wealth · Building | Green | "In 10 years, {chip}. The way I build wealth is ___, and what I'll have at the end of the decade is ___." |
| Self · Self-treatment | Purple | "In 10 years, {chip}. I do this by ___, and in my body it feels like ___." |
| Self · Self-perception | Purple | "In 10 years, {chip}. The story I tell about myself is ___, and the inner voice I hear is ___." |

(Full M2 template table in `Replit_Handoff_Mirror2_Full.md`)

### M3 Template (Under Pressure)

```
"When the {animal} goes shadow, [what I do], [what it costs me], and [what it costs others]."
```

- Three blanks inline (not full-width line breaks like M2)
- Placeholder words: *what I do · what it costs me · what it costs others*
- Filled text renders in burgundy italic Georgia (matching the Under Pressure lens)

### M3 Template (Superpowers · Ship 5.5a)

Mirror of the Shadow sentence — same three-blank architecture, opposite tone (gold in-power vs burgundy in-shadow). Sits at the bottom of every cluster card on the Superpowers room.

```
"In my power, the {animal} [what I do well], [what it gives me], and [what it gives others]."
```

- Three blanks inline (matches Under Pressure architecture)

## 7 · State Logic

### Mirror progression

- Mirror N unlocks when Mirror N-1 is complete (production behavior)
- `?newM[N]=1` URL flag overrides the unlock for testing (dev convenience only — removed before launch)
- Sticky localStorage opt-in: once flag is set, subsequent loads keep mirror unlocked unless `?newM[N]=0` explicitly turns it off

### Room progression within a Mirror

- Room N+1 unlocks when Room N is complete
- Locked rooms are tap-inert: no click handler, no veil, no overlay
- Locked-state copy: *"Unlocks after [previous room name]"*

### "Complete" derivation rules

| Room type | Derivation |
|---|---|
| Pick-style (Your Gifts) | `pickedCount >= 1` (soft) |
| Shape-style (Animals as Anchors) | All clusters: name + animal + 2 descriptors + ≥1 gift |
| Under Pressure | Every picked gift has ≥1 shadow + 1 primary flagged · 3-blank sentence filled per cluster |
| Declaration / Seal | User has claimed (clicked the claim button at least once) |

### State sync on re-entry

- Rooms rebuild from current state on every entry — never trust cached state from prior visits
- Manual edits (cluster names, animals, descriptors, picked shadow chips) preserved when underlying gift selection changes
- Stale entries (shadows for gifts no longer picked) pruned automatically

## 8 · Constants Architecture (mirror3Constants.ts pattern)

When shipping content + a feature gate, **both must land in the same ship**:

```typescript
// Content
SHADOW_LIBRARY[gift.label] = ["word1", "word2", "word3", "word4"]

// Gate
SHADOWS_AVAILABLE[theme] = true
```

**Lesson from M3 Ship 5:** content was added but gate stayed `false`, hiding all the new content. Always pair content updates with their gates.

### Pattern for future feature-gated content

1. Write content into the constant array/object
2. Flip any associated `*_AVAILABLE` boolean to `true`
3. Update the comment above the constant to reflect new coverage
4. Verify with grep that no other gate references the old `false` state

## 11 · Personalization (Ship 5.5d)

**Principle:** The user's name belongs in voice-sensitive copy where it lands like a person speaking to them by name. Where a name would feel performative or formal, second-person ("you" / "your") is the fallback — never the literal word `"User"`.

### Source of truth

- **Lookup helper:** `resolveDisplayName(intakeName, authUser)` from `artifacts/true-ownership/src/lib/displayName.ts`
- **Resolution order:** intake-typed name → `/api/auth/me` `firstName` → `/api/auth/me` `lastName` → `null`
- **Auth payload:** `/api/auth/me` returns `{ user: { id, email, firstName, lastName, profileImageUrl, role } }` (see `artifacts/api-server/src/routes/authPassword.ts`)
- No new schema work is required — `firstName` / `lastName` already exist on `users`. If a future ship introduces a true `displayName` column, update `getDisplayName` and the resolution order stays the same.

### Rules

1. **Never render the literal word `"User"`** anywhere user-facing — JSX, button labels, toast/banner text, page titles, PDF exports, filenames.
2. **Where a name lands well** (greetings, claims, PDF header, "X's Map of Me"): use the resolved name. When `null`, fall back to second person ("you" / "your") or drop the personalized line entirely — never insert the literal `"User"`.
3. **Filenames specifically:** when no name is set, use `"My"` as the stem (e.g. `My-map-of-me.pdf`), not `"User"`. The PDF title likewise reads `"My Map of Me™"` rather than `"My's Map of Me"` (drop the possessive in fallback).
4. **Claude system prompts:** when the prompt embeds the user's name to shape voice, use the resolved name. When `null`, fall back to `"The person"` — never `"The user"`. (Model-facing strings still affect output quality, so they follow the same rule as user-facing strings.)
5. **Greeting pattern:** *"Welcome back, {name}"* when a name is set, *"Welcome back"* when not. No comma when no name.

### Scope audit (Ship 5.5d Phase 1, May 2026)

A scoped grep against `artifacts/true-ownership/src/**` (user-visible strings only — excluding comments, var names, TS types, Anthropic role markers, and dev artifacts) surfaced exactly **one** literal `"User"` site (PDF header fallback in `MapDownloadSection`) plus three Claude system-prompt `"The user"` fallbacks. All four were classified and fixed in Phase 2. Future ships should re-run the same scoped grep when adding rendered copy.

## 12 · Variable-Count Templates (Ship 5.5c)

**Principle:** Surfaces that compose grammar from a variable list (1–5 items) follow a single template family. Spelled-out numerals for human counts, Oxford comma at and-above 3, singular grammar collapsed at 1.

### Own It seal — creature count 1–5

**Subhead** (italic serif, centered, 18px):

| Count | Sentence |
|---|---|
| 1 | *"One creature. It carries gifts in you. It has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."* |
| 2 | *"Two creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."* |
| 3 | *"Three creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."* |
| 4 | *"Four creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."* |
| 5 | *"Five creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."* |

- Spelled-out numeral (`Two`), never `2`
- Singular collapse at count 1: *"It carries / It has"* (not *"Each carries / Each has"*)

**Big Claim label** (uppercase tracked, centered):
- 1 creature → `THE CLAIM`
- 2+ creatures → `THE BIG CLAIM`

**Power roll-up** (italic serif gold-bright, 21px, weight 600, centered):

| Count | Sentence |
|---|---|
| 1 | *"In my power, I am the {c1}."* |
| 2 | *"In my power, I am the {c1} and the {c2}."* |
| 3 | *"In my power, I am the {c1}, the {c2}, and the {c3}."* |
| 4 | *"In my power, I am the {c1}, the {c2}, the {c3}, and the {c4}."* |
| 5 | *"In my power, I am the {c1}, the {c2}, the {c3}, the {c4}, and the {c5}."* |

**Shadow roll-up** (italic serif burgundy, 17px, centered) — composes from each creature's primary shadow word (or first picked if no primary):

| Count | Sentence |
|---|---|
| 1 | *"And when I go shadow, I {s1}."* |
| 2 | *"And when I go shadow, I {s1} and I {s2}."* |
| 3+ | *"And when I go shadow, I {s1}, I {s2}, and I {s3}…"* (Oxford comma) |

**Final line** (serif gold, 22px, weight 600, centered, with top border): *"Both are mine. Owning both is how I come home."* — fixed string, no variable count.

### Per-creature triptych pronouns (Ship 5.5c)

Each creature's grid headers use the creature's pronoun: *"in {pronoun} shadow"* (lowercase italic burgundy) / *"IN {PRONOUN} POWER"* (uppercase tracked gold).

- Pronoun lookup: `getAnimalPronoun(animal)` in `mirror3Constants.ts` — returns `"his" | "her" | "its"`
- Conservative defaults: Lion / Stag → `his` · Lioness / Doe → `her` · all other library animals + all custom (✨) creatures → `its`

### Reusing this pattern

Any future surface that composes a sentence from a 1–5 list (Values roll-up, Behaviors roll-up, etc.) follows the same three rules:

1. **Spelled-out numerals** for human-readable counts
2. **Oxford comma** at 3 and above; bare *"and"* at 2; bare item at 1
3. **Singular grammar collapse** at 1 — every plural verb/pronoun in the template needs a singular twin
