Item 1 — Bug fix: Shadow rows don't sync when picked gifts change
Reproducer: Walk to Under Pressure with a set of picked gifts. Go back to Pick. Deselect some gifts, pick different ones. Return to Under Pressure. The shadow rows still show the OLD gifts.
Expected behavior:

Under Pressure rebuilds the per-cluster gift list on every entry from current state.mirror3.pickedGifts
Gifts no longer picked → their shadow rows disappear (clear state.mirror3.shadows entries for those gift IDs)
Newly added gifts → fresh shadow rows render (Mind/Heart with suggestions, Spine/Spark/Hands with + add)
Previously-filled shadow chips for gifts that ARE still picked → preserve user's work, don't wipe

Fix likely sits in NewA1M3UnderPressureView initialization — derive rows fresh from current state on mount instead of trusting cached state from prior entries.
