## *From Owned to Owner* — The Full Spine

## What this program is

True Ownership is a 45-day evergreen program for anyone in transition — divorce, retirement, empty nesters, career change, identity shifts. Optional live cohorts run alongside. Business owners can extend +45 days to unlock the **Boardroom** (the separate business-strategy app at ag45-owners-circle.com).

The user moves through five disciplines (A1 → A5), each one a small ritual of becoming. The product feels less like a productivity app and more like a guided practice. **Katherine is the voice, the user is the work, the Map of Me is the artifact.**

The brand thread: ***"From Owned to Owner."*** Every A is about reclaiming a piece of yourself.

---

## The journey at a glance

| | Discipline | Outcome the user leaves with |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | All In It · The Commitment | A claimed picture of who they are and who they're becoming |
| **A2** | Align With It · Clarity + Action | 3 priorities, time honestly audited, 1 immediate change |
| **A3** | Advocate For It · Environment | Knows who/what supports them, what to drop, what to add |
| **A4** | Act On It · Quarterly Goals | Clear, simple quarterly goals moving toward who they're becoming |
| **A5** | Accountable To It · Rhythm | Simple scorecard, cadence, accountability partner |

Sequential by default. Each A unlocks after the previous is meaningfully complete. The user can revisit any completed A at any time.

---

## The persistent layer

Two things travel with the user across every A:

1. **My Map of Me™** — a button in the top header, always visible. Builds as work gets done. Empty sections don't show; completed ones do. By the end of A5, it's a complete portrait.

2. **Katherine** — the voice in every Mirror. Inside the room she guides via short videos + prompts. On Output pages she's quietly there via a chat affordance.

## The interaction model — three surfaces

### Surface 1 · A1-A5 grids
The home of each A. A grid of **Mirror cards**, each Mirror = one exercise area. Each card shows its state (locked / current / in-progress / complete) and a single primary action ("Explore with Katherine →" or "Continue →" or "Review →").

### Surface 2 · Explore with Katherine
Click a Mirror card → land on its Explore page. Shows: Katherine's intro video (collapsed by default), and a list of **exercise rows**. Each row has lock/active/complete state. Click an active row → opens the room.

**Never called "Workbook" — always "Explore with Katherine."**

### Surface 3 · The Room (View A · card on dimmed bg)
Click an exercise → the Explore page dims and blurs. A gold-bordered card floats centered. The room contains:
- Breadcrumb at top (*Purpose › Exercise name*)
- Video pause banner ("Pause the video — Katherine sent you here")
- Sub-progress pips for multi-step exercises
- The question, in serif Georgia
- The input (textarea, chips, sentence completion, peel layers)
- Action row (Next / Save / Back)
- Close × in top right

The page behind stays faintly visible (blurred) so the user never forgets where they are.

**The veil transition** plays on entry: a gold-tinted veil rises from below over ~600ms, italic serif text holds at center for ~1400ms (*"Stepping into the room with Katherine…"* / per-exercise variant), then the veil peels away from the top over ~700ms. Total ~2.7 seconds. The room itself fades in during the hold.

### Surface 4 · Museum Placard
When a Mirror is complete, clicking Review on its card opens the Museum Placard — a calm, finished read of what they declared. Centered. Italic serif. Gold rules above and below. The Map of Me extracts from this.

## A1 · All In It · The Commitment

**Outcome:** A claimed picture of who they are and who they're becoming.

**4 Mirrors:**

### Mirror 1 · Purpose

**Outcome:** One declared Purpose statement (italic, short phrase).

**4 exercises in the room:**

#### Ex 1 — Owned Symptoms (burgundy · the cost)
Two sub-steps, no scrolling:
- **Step 1 of 2** — Symptom multi-select (8 chips: "I'm tired but can't tell why", "I say yes when I mean no", "I shrink in certain rooms", "My calendar runs me", "I'm performing, not living", "I keep waiting for permission", "I scroll instead of feel", "I'm successful and empty") + free-text "What else?"
- **Step 2 of 2** — *"If you could remove ONE thing right now and reclaim your life — what would it be?"* Single textarea.

#### Ex 2 — True Ownership (gold · the vision)
**6 questions, mixed cadence, one at a time M1-style with sub-progress pips.**

User's "single thing" from Ex 1 surfaces as a **burgundy callout above Q1** — *"You named your one thing: '[their words].' Now imagine life without it."*

All 6 questions phrased: ***"Describe what life would be like if you…"***

1. **Owned your time** — feeling chip only (presets: Spacious, Unhurried, Energized, Free, Sovereign, At ease) + custom "+ Other…" chip that becomes a text input
2. **Prioritized what mattered to you** — heavy textarea
3. **Achieved the results you wanted** — feeling chip only (Proud, Lit up, Calm, At peace, Whole, Lifted) — no custom
4. **Enjoyed the journey** — feeling chip + custom (Playful, Curious, Alive, In flow, Awake, Light) + Other
5. **Felt aligned on the inside** — sentence completion: *"When I'm aligned, I notice ____."* / *"And the thing I no longer do is ____."*
6. **Set and managed boundaries** — heavy textarea (final landing)

Rhythm: light → heavy → light → light → light → heavy. Lands heavy.

#### Ex 3 — The Peel (cyan · the work)
Multi-layer peeling. Open-ended. Prompts in order: *"When are you most alive?"* → *"So what does that give you?"* → *"And what does THAT give you?"* → *"Underneath that — what's the deepest version?"* → *"One more level — what's the essence?"*

Each layer's answer becomes a gold milestone in the room. After 2+ layers, a *"This is my bottom — declare"* button appears alongside *"Keep peeling — go deeper."*

#### Ex 4 — Declare (gold · claimed)
Single-line italic-serif input centered, pre-filled with last peel layer. The user refines to one phrase. *"This becomes your Purpose in Map of Me."*

### Mirror 2 · BAG · 10-Year Vision

**Outcome:** 3 visionary identity statements — who I've become as an owner in 10 years.

**Opens with the Two Futures exercise** (this is the M1 "Person A vs Person B" you love). Then 3 visionary statements one at a time.

5 exercises in the room (proposed — TO BE FINALIZED IN NEXT SESSION):
1. **Two Futures · A** — Describe the version of you in 10 years if nothing changes. (Burgundy tone — the cost of staying.)
2. **Two Futures · B** — Describe the version of you in 10 years if you become the owner. (Gold — the vision.)
3. **Time vision** — In 10 years, my relationship with time is… (gold)
4. **Wealth vision** — In 10 years, my wealth is… (gold)
5. **Growth vision** — In 10 years, who I've become is… (gold)

### Mirror 3 · Identity · Superpowers

**Outcome:** 3 superpowers named, with what they give the user.

**4 exercises** (proposed):
1. Qualities — what people thank you for that you didn't realize was hard
2. Under pressure — what shows up in you when things get hard
3. The 3 superpowers (3 sub-slots)
4. What each gives you (per superpower)

### Mirror 4 · Values · Behaviors

**Outcome:** 3-5 values paired with the behaviors that prove them, with a hierarchy.

**5 exercises** (proposed):
1. **IRK** — what irritates, repels, would make you walk away. (Negative space reveals values.)
2. Standards — what does that mean you require?
3. Name your values (3-5 chips/short inputs)
4. For each value — the behavior that proves it
5. Hierarchy — when two values conflict, which wins?

### What's cut from current Replit A1
- Direction · 3-YEAR (moves to A4)
- Direction · 1-YEAR (moves to A4)
- Introduction screen (becomes A1 video, collapsed by default)

## A2 · Align With It · Clarity + Action

**Outcome:** 3 priorities, time audited, 1 immediate change committed.

**5 Mirrors** (high-level, not yet designed in detail):
1. **Growth Alignment** — score how fully you're living your A1 Identity & Values today (1-10 sliders)
2. **Choose 3 Priorities** — based on what alignment surfaced + your BAG. The 3 that thread through the rest of the program.
3. **Time Audit** — 4 phases: Name your week → Sort by priority + feeling → What's missing → See it
4. **Key Misalignment** — 1-2 specific gaps named
5. **The One Thing** — one immediate behavior change this week

**Cut from existing Replit A2:**
- Direction Alignment (redundant once priorities + audit do the work)
- Future Self Letter (eliminated)
- A2 Mirror as a separate step (absorbed into Map of Me)

## A3 · Advocate For It · Environment

**Outcome:** Knows who/what supports them, what to drop, what to add.

**Should feel like the M-prototype energy** — more reflective, more visual vitality, more guided pacing.

**5 Mirrors:**
1. **People · Advocates & Anchors** — list + tag each person
2. **Environment** — physical/digital supports vs drains
3. **Thoughts** — inner advocates and anchors
4. **What to Drop / What to Add** — synthesis (one of each)
5. **The Board** — 3-7 imagined advisors

---

## A4 · Act On It · Quarterly Goals

**Outcome:** Clear simple quarterly goals tied to the 3 priorities from A2.

**4 Mirrors:**
1. **3-Year Outcomes** — one sentence per priority (3 sentences). *"In 3 years, my [priority] is…"*
2. **1-Year Outcomes** — one sentence per priority. *"This year, my [priority] = …"*
3. **The Structural One Thing** per priority — what you're building this year
4. **Quarterly Action Map** — first 45-day move per priority

---

## A5 · Accountable To It · Rhythm

**Outcome:** Simple scorecard, cadence, accountability partner.

**3 Mirrors:**
1. **Scorecard** — 3 priorities + 1-Year outcomes + how progress gets measured. One signal per priority.
2. **Rhythm** — weekly pulse / monthly adjust / quarterly reset. Lightweight time pulse tucked into the quarterly check-in.
3. **Accountability Partner** — name one human + set the cadence with them.

## Design system (locked)

### Color semantics — applied consistently everywhere

| Color | Hex (dark / light) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | #C9A24B / #B8892F | Truth, declared, honored, yours |
| **Burgundy** | #A03B3B / #7A2E2E | Cost, gravity, mirror, what it costs you |
| **Cyan** | #4fcbf2 / #2a8fb5 | Working energy, in progress, current focus |
| **Green** | #22c55e / #16a34a | Quiet completion |
| **Orange** | #f59e0b / #d97706 | Tension, attention needed |
| **Red** | #ef4444 / #dc2626 | Stop, refuse, disowned (M1 "Completely Owned" red) |
| **Purple** | #8b5cf6 / #7c3aed | Competing forces (sparingly) |

**Principle:** *Gold honors what's true. Burgundy names what's costing you. Cyan is working energy.*

### Tokens

| | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| Background | #0a0a0a | #f5f3ef |
| Card surface | #111113 | #ffffff |
| Soft surface | #1a1a1c | #ebe7df |
| Text primary | #f0ece4 | #1a1a1a |
| Text secondary | #9a9590 | #5c5752 |
| Text mute | #5c5752 | #9a9590 |
| Border | #2a2826 | #e0ddd8 |
| Border soft | #1f1d1b | #ece8df |

### Typography
- **Body:** DM Sans
- **Headings + serif body:** Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif
- **Declared/contemplative text:** Italic Georgia
- **Eyebrows:** 10-11px, 700 weight, 0.18-0.22em letter-spacing, uppercase, gold

### Room style (View A · locked)
- Centered card on dimmed/blurred Explore page (filter: blur(2px); opacity 0.32)
- Gold 1px border, 80px gold-glow shadow
- Padding 80px top / 24px sides / 40px bottom
- Card max-width 720px
- Always shows breadcrumb at top of card (*Purpose › Exercise name*)
- × close button top right
- Card fades + lifts in over 500ms (cubic-bezier ease)

### Veil transition (entering each exercise)
- Stage 1 — Rise (600ms): radial gold-tinted veil sweeps up from bottom (cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1))
- Stage 2 — Text fade (starts 400ms into rise, 500ms duration): italic serif Georgia line rises into view + fades in
- Stage 3 — HOLD (1400ms): user reads the threshold line + screen swap happens invisibly under the held veil
- Stage 4 — Text fade-out (300ms)
- Stage 5 — Fall (700ms): veil peels back from top (cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1))
- **Total ~2.7 seconds.** Critical: the HOLD is what makes it feel like a threshold, not a flash.

**Per-exercise threshold text** (italic serif gold):
- Owned Symptoms → *"Stepping into the room with Katherine…"*
- True Ownership → *"Imagining the owner…"*
- The Peel → *"Going deeper with Katherine…"*
- Declare → *"The moment of claiming…"*

### Mobile (≤900px)
- Nav pills hide behind hamburger
- Map button shows just gold dot, label hides
- Card grids collapse to single column
- Room expands to nearly full-screen (95vh max)
- Textareas remain large and tappable
- Threshold text size 20px (down from 26px)

### Theme toggle
Persistent in header. Dark ↔ Light. Preference saved per user.

## The Workbook (Room) data model

Each Mirror has its own room state. State persists across sessions (auto-save).

```js
// Per-mirror state
state.purpose = {
  symptoms: {
    picks: ['I scroll instead of feel', ...],  // array of selected chips
    note: '',                                   // free-text addition
    holdingBack: ''                             // the single thing
  },
  trueOwnership: {
    answers: [
      { type: 'feeling-only', feeling: 'Spacious' },
      { type: 'heavy', text: '...' },
      { type: 'feeling-only', feeling: 'Lit up' },
      { type: 'feeling-only', feeling: 'My own feeling' },
      { type: 'completion', c1: '...', c2: '...' },
      { type: 'heavy', text: '...' }
    ]
  },
  peel: {
    layers: [
      { prompt: 'When are you most alive?', answer: '...' },
      { prompt: 'So what does that give you?', answer: '...' },
      ...
    ]
  },
  declaration: 'Luminal leader.'
};

state.bag = { twoFutures: {...}, time: '...', wealth: '...', growth: '...' };
state.identity = { qualities: '...', underPressure: '...', superpowers: [...] };
state.values = { irk: '...', standards: '...', values: [...], behaviors: {...}, hierarchy: [...] };
```

### Exercise unlock rules
- An exercise unlocks when the previous one is **complete**
- An exercise is **complete** when its primary required output is non-empty
- A Mirror is complete when all its exercises are complete
- A Mirror's status: Locked → Up next (current) → In progress → Complete
- Once complete, the exercise row stays clickable (for edit)

### Edit anytime
- Clicking a complete exercise row opens that exercise in the room
- All previous answers pre-filled
- The Save button now says "Save changes" instead of "Save & continue"
- The video pause banner now says "You already explored this · edit anytime"
- No separate "review mode" — just type into the same inputs

## Operating principles (carry-over from prior sessions)

- Katherine works long sessions; does not want to be prompted to stop
- Mobile-responsive locked
- Replit Agent over-engineers when scope is ambiguous — keep handoffs narrow
- Test on dev (spock.replit.dev) before redeploying to prod (ag45-owners-circle.com)
- Fork the True Ownership Replit project as a snapshot BEFORE making changes
- File chunks under ~3KB to avoid Replit silent truncation
- For multi-line edits, paste-at-cursor (not Cmd+H which doesn't handle multi-line on Katherine's setup)
