## The model (canonical — applies to every A, every mirror)

The hierarchy is: **A (discipline) → Mirror → Section → Exercises.**

### Gating
1. **A-level:** The next A cannot be started until the previous A is fully complete. A locked A is VISIBLE but locked, and its locked card names where to go (pattern already built in #37: "Go to All In It →"). A1 → A2 → A3 → A4 → A5 in order.
2. **Mirror-level:** Within an A, mirrors are sequential. Mirror N+1 is locked until Mirror N is complete/claimed. Locked mirror cards show their unlock condition (already the pattern: "Unlocks after [previous mirror]").
3. **Section-level:** Within a mirror, sections are the rungs on the ladder. They unlock in order.

### The ladder (home base)
- Each mirror opens to its vertical step list — the LADDER — listing its SECTIONS with status (Up next / In progress / Complete / Open · tap to revisit / Sealed for the Claim rung).
- The ladder is where the user lives BETWEEN sections.
- Example (Purpose): Owned Symptoms → True Ownership → The Peel → Declare. Example (Values): Discover → Behaviors → Rank → Claim. (Each of these list items is a SECTION.)

### Inside a section
- A section may contain one or more EXERCISES.
- Exercises WITHIN a section auto-flow: Exercise 1 → Exercise 2 → Exercise 3, advancing forward inside the section without returning to the ladder.
- ONLY when the whole section is finished does the room close and return the user to the LADDER, with that section marked Complete and the next section shown as the active rung.
- The user taps the next rung themselves. No auto-jump from one section to the next.

### Progress indicators inside a section
- KEEP: the contextual in-step line, e.g. "MIRROR 1 · EXERCISE 1 · OWNED SYMPTOMS" + "STEP 1 OF 2". This orients the user inside the current section's exercise sub-flow. It stays on every mirror.
- REMOVE: the horizontal dots-and-connectors stepper at the top of the room (e.g. "Discover 1 — Behaviors 2 — Rank 3 — Claim ✓"). Remove from ALL mirrors in BOTH A1 and A2. It duplicates the ladder and competes with the in-step counter.

### The one forward-advance exception
The final Claim section, after claiming, advances forward OUT of the mirror to the next mirror (or, if last mirror, completes the A). This is leaving the mirror, not moving between sections within it — so it keeps a forward CTA ("Continue to [Next Mirror] →").
