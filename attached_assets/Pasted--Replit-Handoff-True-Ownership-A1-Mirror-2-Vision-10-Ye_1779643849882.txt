### The 11 domains and 44 chips (master table)

Chips are written as feeling+reality phrases. Each chip is mapped to a tier (1-4) where Tier 1 = Completely Owned, Tier 2 = Complacent, Tier 3 = Aspiring, Tier 4 = True Owner.

**CRITICAL:** Chip display order is RANDOMIZED per domain on each render. Use `${domainId}-${lens}-${pass}-${userId}` as a deterministic seed so the order is stable within a session but anti-anchored across users.

#### Lens 1 — Time (5 domains)

| Domain | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Work | My calendar runs and drains me | I'm coasting at work | Mostly fulfilling, some leaks | I own my work time and it fulfills me |
| Family | Little engagement | There, but distracted | Half-present | Full and present |
| Friends | I've lost touch | Surface-level only | Tended but thin | Rich and current |
| Hobbies | I've forgotten what I love | I make excuses | Squeezed in when I can | Lit up by what I make time for |
| Health | Running on fumes | Surviving, not thriving | Inconsistent but trying | Strong and rested |

#### Lens 2 — Wealth (3 domains)

| Domain | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cashflow | Money is tight, I'm scrambling | Stable, but stuck | Generating, with margin | Abundant and flowing |
| Building | Surviving, nothing saved | Saving when I can | Building, no real plan | Compounding on purpose |
| Stability | One bad month from disaster | Treading water | Solid, but precarious | Rooted and resilient |

#### Lens 3 — Self (3 domains)

| Domain | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-treatment | I'm cruel to myself | I tolerate myself | Mostly kind to myself | I treat myself like someone I love |
| Self-perception | I don't recognize who I am | I know the role, not the person | Becoming someone I recognize | I am who I am, fully |
| Becoming | I'm the version of me from years ago | I change reluctantly | Shifting, with friction | Becoming, with grace |

### Strict completion validation

Continue button is DISABLED until every domain on the current screen has a chip selected.

- **Disabled visual:** background `#1f3550`, text `#5c7088`, cursor not-allowed
- **Enabled visual:** background `#A03B3B` (Future A) or `#C9A24B` (Future B), text appropriate, hover lifts brightness 1.08

**Highlight what's missing:** When user attempts to click Continue while disabled OR hovers it for >500ms:
1. Scroll any incomplete domain into view
2. Pulse the domain name in the tone color for ~1.5s (text-color shift over 600ms × 2 cycles)
3. Tooltip near Continue: "Pick a chip for each domain to continue." (auto-dismiss 3s)

#### Compound line logic

For each Future A chip the user picked:
- Tier 1 chip → no compound line (already at bottom)
- Tier 2 chip → "{Tier 2 chip phrase, sentence-cased} becomes {Tier 1 chip phrase from same domain, sentence-cased}"
- Tier 3 chip → "{Tier 3 phrase} becomes {Tier 2 phrase from same domain}"
- Tier 4 chip → no compound line (already at top)

**Cap at 4 compound lines max.** Pick the 4 from lowest tier first, then by domain order (Time → Wealth → Self).

#### Lowest-tier chips list (below Block 1)

Render 3-4 of the user's Tier 1 or Tier 2 picks inline, separated by periods. Greyed text. Example: *"Coasting at work. Half-present. Saving when I can. I tolerate myself."*

If user has no Tier 1 or Tier 2 picks, use Tier 3 picks. If all Tier 4 (extremely rare), single line: *"You're already doing well — but the work isn't done."*

#### "OneThing restated" logic (v1)

The dagger uses `{oneThing}` literally, in quotes, inside the phrasing:

> *The way you keep "{oneThing}" — it owns you.*

The literal quote reads slightly awkward grammatically but ships safely. A v2 iteration could LLM-restate; v1 uses the quote.

## Prose room shared mechanics (Exercises 2, 3, 4)

### Auto-save behavior

- **On blur (or 600ms debounce while typing):** save to server
- **On Continue tap:** save all blanks + completion flag, advance
- **On server save fail:** fallback to sessionStorage, retry on next save attempt
- **Reuses Mirror 1's existing auto-save engine** — do not write new infrastructure
