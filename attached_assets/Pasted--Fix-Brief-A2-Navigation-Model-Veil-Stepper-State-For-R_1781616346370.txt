## Context

Fix these in order. Plan before every change. Do not bundle fixes.

## Navigation Model Decision (read this first)

**The A1 return-to-stepper model is the standard for all mirrors going forward.**

### What this means

**A1 model (correct — adopt everywhere):**
Hub card → tap "Begin" / "Resume" / "Sealed · tap to revisit" → veil fires → step list opens → user taps step 1 → exercise room opens → user completes it → lands back on step list → user taps step 2 → exercise room opens → completes → back to step list → etc.

The step list is the home base. The user returns to it between every step. They always see their full progress and consciously choose to continue.

**A2 model (wrong — remove this):**
Hub card → intermediate overview screen (separate from hub) → enters step 1 → "Continue to [next step] →" button auto-advances to step 2 without returning to step list → step 3 → etc.

The user is pushed through steps without ever returning to see the full picture.

### Two things to remove
1. The **horizontal dot indicator** inside the exercise room (the dots + connectors + labels at the top of the modal, e.g. "1 Your priorities ── ✓ Where time goes ── ✓ What's missing ── 4 Claim"). Remove this from all A2 mirror rooms. It is redundant when the step list is the home base.
2. The **"Continue to [next step] →" auto-advance button** at the bottom of each exercise. Replace with a "Done → Back to [Mirror Name]" button that returns the user to the step list on completion.

### What stays exactly as-is in A1
Do not touch A1. It already uses this model correctly. A1 is the reference.

### What the step list must look like (already correct in A2, just has a status bug — see Fix 3)
- Video intro row at top (collapsible)
- Numbered step rows: step name + status
- Status states: **In progress** (gold, current) / **Complete ✓** (gold checkmark) / **Open · tap to revisit** (muted) / **Sealed · tap to revisit ✓** (gold checkmark, for Claim row)
- Tapping any row opens that step's exercise room
- "← Back to A2" button at bottom left

## Fix 1 — Remove horizontal dot indicator from A2 exercise rooms

### What to remove
The horizontal stepper component at the top of every A2 exercise room modal — the row of numbered dots connected by lines, showing step names and gold checkmarks. This appears in M1 (Alignment Check), M2 (Time Audit), and M3 (Named Truth) exercise rooms.

### What to preserve
Everything else in the room is untouched — the breadcrumb ("Time Audit › Your priorities"), the × close button, the exercise content, the CTA button.

### Acceptance criteria
- No horizontal dot/connector/label row appears inside any A2 exercise room
- A1 rooms are untouched (they never had this component)

---

## Fix 2 — Replace "Continue to [next step]" with "Done → Back to [Mirror]"

### What is broken
Each A2 exercise room has a "Continue to [next step] →" button at the bottom that auto-advances to the next step without returning to the step list. This bypasses the step list entirely.

### What it must do instead
On completing an exercise, the primary CTA returns the user to the step list for that mirror. The step list then shows the completed step as "Complete ✓" and the next step as the active one.

### Button labels
- **Mid-mirror steps:** "Done → Back to Time Audit" (or whichever mirror name)
- **Final step before Claim:** same pattern — "Done → Back to [Mirror Name]" — the Claim row on the step list is then the next tap
- **Claim step:** after claiming, "Continue to [Next Mirror] →" remains — this is the one forward-advance that stays, because it exits the mirror entirely

### Acceptance criteria
- Completing any non-Claim step returns user to the step list
- Step list immediately shows that step as "Complete ✓"
- The next step is visually active/highlighted as the logical next action
- Claim step CTA remains as a forward advance to next mirror (unchanged)
- A1 is untouched

## Fix 3 — Step list shows wrong status on mount

### What is broken
When landing on the A2 M2 Time Audit step list after the mirror is fully sealed, steps 1–3 show as "In progress" / "Open" instead of "Complete ✓". They only update to "Complete" after visiting the Claim screen and returning. This is a derived-state hydration bug — the step list is not reading completion state correctly on mount.

### What it must do
On mount, the step list reads the actual completion boolean for each step from state and renders the correct status immediately — no interaction required to trigger the correct display.

- A step is **Complete ✓** when its completion boolean is `true` in state
- A step is **In progress** when it is the current active step (partially done)
- A step is **Open · tap to revisit** when not yet started
- The Claim row is **Sealed · tap to revisit ✓** when `claimed === true`

"Visited" is not the same as "complete." A step that has been opened but not finished is "In progress" for the current step, or "Open" for future steps — not "Complete."

### Acceptance criteria
- Landing on any A2 mirror step list shows correct status for all steps immediately on mount
- A fully sealed mirror shows all steps as "Complete ✓" and Claim as "Sealed · tap to revisit ✓" — no interaction needed
- A partially complete mirror shows done steps as "Complete ✓", current step as "In progress", future steps as "Open"

## Fix 4 — Veil not firing on re-entry to sealed A2 mirrors

### What is broken
The veil fires on first entry to A2 M2 and M3. It does NOT fire when re-entering a mirror that is already claimed/sealed — tapping "Sealed · tap to revisit →" goes straight to the step list with no veil.

A1 Purpose correctly fires the veil on every entry including revisits. A2 must match.

### What it must do
The veil fires every time the user enters a mirror from the hub card button — fresh, in-progress, or sealed. Claimed state does not suppress the veil. The rule is: veil fires on hub-card-to-mirror entry. It does not fire on step-to-step-list transitions within the mirror.

### Veil texts (confirm these are wired)
- **M1 Alignment Check** entry: *"Looking at where you stand right now…"*
- **M2 Time Audit** entry: *"Looking at where your time actually goes…"*
- **M3 Named Truth** entry: *"Looking at what the truth is showing you…"*
- **M3 Step 2 ("Rethink your time")** entry from step list: *"This is where it shifts…"*
- **M3 Claim** entry from step list: *"The moment of naming it…"*

### Root cause to check
1. Is the veil component mounted inside the A2 hub render tree at all? (Prior session noted it may never have been mounted in `NewA2HubView`)
2. Is the veil trigger condition gated on `!claimed`? Remove that gate.
3. The veil must re-trigger on every hub-card tap — use a key or counter to force remount if needed

### Veil appearance
- Background: warm cream — NOT grey (match A1 veil exactly)
- Text: italic Georgia, gold, centred
- Timing: reuse `NA1_VEIL_TIMING` exactly — do not create new constants

### Acceptance criteria
- Veil fires on every entry to M1, M2, M3 from hub card — fresh, in-progress, and sealed
- Veil fires on M3 step 2 and Claim step entries from the step list
- Veil does NOT fire when returning to the step list after completing a step
- Veil does NOT fire when tapping between steps on the step list
- Veil background is warm cream, not grey
