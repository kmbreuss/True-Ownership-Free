## 2. Architecture & consistency — READ BEFORE BUILDING (critical)

This is the whole point of the ship. **Reuse; do not reinvent.**

- **Cards:** the A3 hub cards must be the **same component and styles as the A1 mirror-hub cards.** Do not build a new card. Whatever A1 uses for number, state tag, MIRROR label, title, description, progress pips, status line, CTA, and footer meta — reuse it.
- **Room shell:** the A3 section rooms must reuse the **A2 section-room shell** — top header (brand + A1–A5 pills + account + theme toggle), the **220px left section sidebar** (section list with gold-highlighted active item + VOICE dropdown), and the room content column. Do not build a new shell.
- **Fonts — CONFIRMED against the live app (read directly):**
  - Body / labels / buttons / eyebrow / sidebar = **DM Sans** (button text color `#1A1407`).
  - **A3 hub cards + hub hero = Georgia**, matching A1 exactly. Reference: A1's card title "Purpose" is `Georgia` 21px / weight 600 / `#2A2620`; the "01" is Georgia *italic* 10px in gold `#b8892f`; the hero "Commit to the life you want to live." is Georgia 36px / 600.
  - **A3 section-room heroes/quotes = Cormorant Garamond** (the existing `--serif` variable), matching A2's rooms.
  - Net rule: **build the A3 hub to match A1 (Georgia); build the A3 rooms to match A2 (Cormorant via `--serif`). Introduce no other serif.**
- **Colors / tokens — CONFIRMED:** `--gold` `#b8892f` (light) / `#c9a84c` (dark); light bg `#f5f3ef`; dark bg `#0d1b2e`; `--border` `#1e3554`; burgundy `#7A2E2E` (light) / `#A03B3B` (dark); `--rail-w` `220px`; `--na1-radius` `10px`, `--na1-radius-xl` `18px`. **Semantic (locked):** Advocate = gold, Neutral = muted grey, Anchor = burgundy. Add no new palette.
- **Light default + dark toggle** — identical to A1/A2.
- **Mobile — CONFIRMED (reuse the app's existing responsive system; do not re-create it):**
  - **≥768px:** the left spine/rail (`.na1-spine`, width `--rail-w` = 220px) is `position:fixed` and content gets `padding-left:var(--rail-w)`. **<768px:** the rail collapses and content goes full-width.
  - The mirror-card hub grid (`.na1-mirror-grid`) collapses to **1 column at ≤600px** (gap 14px).
  - Room phase layouts (`.na1-phase-body`) collapse to **1 column at ≤640px**; button/action groups stack at ≤600px.
  - Heroes downsize at ≤767px; `.na1-shell` padding tightens at ≤600px.
  - So: build the A3 hub using `.na1-mirror-grid` and the A3 rooms inside the existing shell / phase-body classes — mobile is inherited automatically. Verify at 375px. Introduce **no new breakpoints**; if a genuinely new A3-only component needs one, match the existing set: **768 / 640 / 600**.
- **Operating model / transitions:** hub → tap card → veil transition → room, exactly as A2. Claim ceremony consistent with A1/A2: validate inputs → seal message → Claim → card flips to **Complete** with a gold burst → next mirror unlocks and flashes.

## 3. Ship 1 scope

### 3a. A3 hub (all six cards, gated)

Header/intro block (reuse A1's):
- Eyebrow: `A3 · ADVOCATE FOR IT · [Name]`
- Serif hero (copy TBD — placeholder from mockup: "Surround the life you're building.")
- Italic welcome: "Welcome back, [Name]."
- Lead: "Six mirrors. Audit the people, places, and thoughts around you — then build your board and make one move."
- Progress line: "**0 of 6** complete"

Six cards, in this order, using the A1 card anatomy:

| # | Mirror | Title | One-line description | Ship-1 state |
|---|--------|-------|----------------------|--------------|
| 01 | Mirror 1 | People | The people around you — and how each one shapes your energy. | **Up next** |
| 02 | Mirror 2 | Environment | Your habits and your places — what lifts you, what drains you. | Locked · "Unlocks after People" |
| 03 | Mirror 3 | Thoughts | The voice that runs when no one else is in the room. | Locked · "Unlocks after Environment" |
| 04 | Mirror 4 | Energy Map | Everyone and everything you named, by how close you keep it. | Locked · "Unlocks after Thoughts" |
| 05 | Mirror 5 | The Board | The people you want in your corner — name the roles, seat who fits. | Locked · "Unlocks after Energy Map" |
| 06 | Mirror 6 | The Move | The single thing to act on now. Hand it to A4. | Locked · "Unlocks after The Board" |

Only **People** is functional this ship; the rest are locked cards (no room behind them yet).

### 3b. Mirror 1 — People (full room)

Reuse the A2 room shell. Flow, copy, and interaction per the mockup:

**Phase 1 — Brain dump**
- Serif prompt: "Who's in your inner circle?"
- Lead: "The people who shape your energy — on purpose or by default. Name them first. Rate them second. Don't censor."
- One textarea, one name per line, live counter ("N names · most lists land between 10 and 25").
- "If you're stuck" prompt chips (from mockup): Who did I talk to this week? / Who's in my group chats? / Who do I text when something big happens? / Who do I avoid calling back? / Whose opinion runs in my head?
- Continue enabled once ≥ 1 name.

**Phase 2 — Rate them**
- Serif prompt: "Rate each one — honestly."
- Legend: **Advocate** lifts you · **Neutral** is just there · **Anchor** holds you back.
- Each name → single-select Advocate / Neutral / Anchor.
- **No category/role dropdown. No per-person action.** (Action lives in The Move, a later ship.)
- Continue enabled when all names rated.

**Claim**
- Summary: counts of Advocates / Neutral / Anchors.
- Seal (two variants, per mockup): a gentler "thinner than you hoped" variant when advocates = 0 or anchors > advocates; otherwise the "you looked, that takes courage" variant.
- Claim button → gold burst → People card flips to **Complete** ("Claimed.") → **Environment unlocks** (Up next, flash) → progress "1 of 6".

**Data**
- Persist People as `name → rating` (advocate / neutral / anchor), namespaced to A3, so **The Board** (later ship) can read the advocates. Advocate names are the head-start pool for the Board's seats.
