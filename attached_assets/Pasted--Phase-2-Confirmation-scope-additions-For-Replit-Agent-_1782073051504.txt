## Addition 2 — Re-architect Discover into real ladder SECTIONS (this is the tracking fix)

Decision on the tracking-model question: **go with re-architecture (A), not a counter patch (B).**

The problem: today the Values ladder has 4 rungs (Discover / Behaviors / Rank / Claim) but "Discover" alone hides 8 internal steps tracked by a "STEP X OF 8" counter. That does NOT mirror Purpose, where each stage (Owned Symptoms / True Ownership / The Peel / Declare) is its own ladder rung. The stages inside Discover are genuinely distinct — they deserve to be sections, like Purpose's.

What to do: make the Discover stages real ladder rungs (sections), mirroring Purpose. Build Phase 2's grouping step AS a section on the ladder, not as step-5-of-8 inside a monolithic "Discover." Use the in-section step counter only for genuine micro-steps WITHIN a section (the way Purpose uses "STEP 1 OF 2"), not as a running count across the whole flow. This re-sectioning IS the tracking fix — option B (just relabel the counter) is moot.

Proposed final section list for the Values mirror ladder (post full redesign, Phases 2–4):

  Admire → Irks → Flip the irks → Name the standards → Rank → Claim

Notes:
- "Flip the irks" lands in Phase 3 but reserve its place in the section model now so the ladder doesn't get re-architected twice.
- Portrait: you decide whether it's its own rung or folds into the tail of Admire/Irks as a review step — propose what fits how Portrait is currently wired. Either is fine; just tell us which in the revised plan.
- Each section returns to the ladder when complete; exercises within a section auto-flow; the next section flashes on the ladder — same model as Purpose/A2 (from #39/#40). Nothing flashes when revisiting a fully-complete mirror.
