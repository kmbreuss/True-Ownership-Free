## Core principles (read first)

1. **Gifts, not qualities.** Every user-facing string uses "gift." Never "quality," "trait," "attribute," "strength."

2. **Animals are memory anchors.** Image + descriptor sticks where word lists don't. The animal carries the gift-cluster.

3. **Both sides are theirs.** Gift = identity. Shadow = pattern to watch. The seal's closing line — *"Both are mine. Owning both is how I come home."* — is locked copy.

4. **System suggests, user owns the naming.** Cluster names, animals, chip groupings, and shadow words are all suggestions. User overrides anything. Suggestions are scaffolding.

5. **Read-only seal.** No inputs on the seal. Held moment.

6. **One animal at a time on Step 2 + Step 3.** No long scrolling pages. Continue button moves to next animal.

## STEP 1b · SHAPE YOUR VERSIONS (combined 1b/1c)

**Pip bar now shows 2 dots, not 3** (Pick + Shape).

### Layout (per cluster card)

1. **Cluster name** (suggested from library, editable) + × remove
2. **Animal row** (in a slight inset block):
   - Emoji icon (auto-assigned, tappable to swap)
   - `[descriptor word 1]` `[descriptor word 2]` `[animal name]` — 3 inputs side-by-side
   - `browse →` link (opens curated animal modal scored for this cluster's gifts)
3. **Help row:** *"★ Add two words that capture your {animal} in its power"*
4. **Gifts that creature carries** label + chip bin (tap chip to move it to another cluster)

### Initial state on landing
- Cluster name: PRE-FILLED from suggestion library + `SUGGESTED · TAP TO RENAME` micro-label
- Animal: PRE-FILLED from animal library based on gift pattern
- Emoji: AUTO-ASSIGNED from animal map
- Word 1 + Word 2: **EMPTY** with placeholder text (`word 1`, `word 2`), gold-tinted background to draw eye

### Initial cluster assembly (Step 1a → 1b transition)
1. Take all picked gifts
2. Group by theme (Mind/Heart/Spine/Spark/Hands)
3. Heuristic:
   - 3+ themes present → 3 clusters, one per dominant theme
   - 1-2 themes → split by count
   - <6 gifts → 2 clusters
4. Run cluster name + animal matching on each
5. Render

### Validation to proceed
- Each cluster: name (text) + animal (text) + descriptor word 1 + descriptor word 2 + at least 1 gift
- Cannot proceed if any field empty
- Primary button (`superpowers →`) disabled at 50% opacity until valid

## STEP 3 · UNDER PRESSURE

### Layout pattern: ONE ANIMAL AT A TIME with Continue button

- Pip-subbar shows progress: *"Eagle · 1 of 3"*
- One full-card view per animal (creature name, version label, gift-shadow pairs, shadow story textarea)
- Continue button at bottom: *"Continue to {next animal name} →"*
- On the last animal, button reads *"claim · seal mirror 3 →"*

### Per-gift shadow row
- Left: gift chip (gold)
- Center: `→` arrow
- Right: shadow chip picker
  - 4 system-suggested shadow chips from library
  - Tap to pick (fills burgundy)
  - One can be marked primary via long-press OR tap-twice — gets **burgundy dot marker `#A03B3B`** at left of chip text (NOT gold star)
  - `+ add` reveals inline input for custom shadow word

### Shadow story (per cluster)
- Prompt: *"When the {animal} goes shadow — what does it actually look like in your life?"*
- Free textarea, auto-grow
- Soft validation: 30+ characters preferred, not enforced

### Validation
- Each gift needs ≥1 shadow chip picked
- Each gift needs exactly 1 primary shadow flagged
- Shadow story optional but encouraged

## SEAL

### Layout
- Eyebrow `MIRROR 3 OF 4 · SEAL` + gold ★ divider
- H1 *"What lives in you"*
- Opening line: *"Three creatures. Each carries gifts in you. Each has a shadow. Your gifts are who you are — your shadow is what to watch."*
- Legend: *"Filled chips = gifts you live with · Outlined chips ✦ = gifts emerging in you"*
- **Per-animal triptych** (read-only):
  - Centered animal name (gold italic) + version label (uppercase tracked)
  - Axis header: *"in {her/his/their} shadow"* (left, muted lowercase) | *"IN {HER/HIS/THEIR} POWER"* (right, uppercase gold)
  - Grid: shadow chip (small, quiet, burgundy with dot for primary) | `→` | gift chip (large, gold, bold)
- **Binding sentence** (system-assembled):
  - Power line: *"In my power, I am [the {creature1}, the {creature2}, and the {creature3}]."*
  - Shadow line (smaller, muted): *"In shadow, I drift into [{primary1}, {primary2}, and {primary3}]."*
  - Closing line (gold italic, locked copy): *"Both are mine. Owning both is how I come home."*
- **Katherine seal block** (italic Georgia, gold left border, locked copy)
- Buttons: `← edit anything` / `align with it · A2 →`

### Seal ceremony
- Gold burst on first land + ~360ms plateau
- Content fades in
- If user revisits after edit, full ceremony re-fires + `sealRevisedAt[]` appends timestamp

## A2 INTEGRATION

Per gift, A2 renders:
- Slider with 5 named positions: **Shadow · Slipping · Holding · Growing · In Your Power**
- Internally stored as 1-5 for bottleneck math
- Left anchor: primary shadow word from M3 (with burgundy dot to maintain visual continuity)
- Right anchor: gift name (gold)
- Track gradient: burgundy → gold
- Position label below slider: *"You are {POSITION} with this gift right now."*
- Optional context: *"YOUR WORDS FROM MIRROR 3"* pulls shadow story

After all gifts rated, bottleneck summary:
- Ranked by score (1 = most shadow)
- Closing prompt: *"Which one feels like it would open up the most if you worked here? Pick one — it carries forward into A3."*
