name: Navigation and recordings policy
description: Source-only recordings, access rather than visit history, and phone navigation scope.
---
The recordings library shows only recordings with configured sources that the member can access under the existing stage/mirror rules. Do not show unavailable placeholders or “Later” rows, and do not introduce opening-history tracking in browser or account storage.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaced the initial visit-based library proposal with access-based filtering and source-only rows. The page must not imply that unrecorded material exists or create new member data.

**How to apply:** Populate the shared media inventory when a recording becomes available, preserving its actual journey position and known length. Reuse existing players and permission gates; filling a known source should be sufficient for it to appear. Keep the exact closing copy: “More of Katherine’s recordings are on the way.”

Phone bottom navigation belongs only on Entry Hall, top-level A hubs, Map of Me and Audio. Exercise rooms, Reality Check scenes, ceremonies and full-screen overlays retain their own controls.

**Why:** The owner specifically excluded persistent bottom navigation from guided work and full-screen experiences.

**How to apply:** Keep navigation visibility separate from progress. Global Map entry may keep return context in memory, but must not write new account fields or change the existing ceremony entry/return path.
