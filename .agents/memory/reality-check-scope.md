Reality Check's initial ship must not introduce account/schema/signup changes. Treat real accounts as members; free access is only a DEV, nonpersistent simulation. Real free signup and authoritative account access are deferred.

**Why:** The owner explicitly removed the account/database portion from the initial build authorization to keep it isolated.

**How to apply:** Do not infer permission to add users.access, registration, migrations or paid-discipline write restrictions from the original attached brief or draft plan. Obtain authorization for that later work. RC remains behind the newRC query flag until sign-off.

The Reality Check journey brief supersedes RC-1's per-area named defaults with a flat four-default object in internal payload version 2, retaining the existing storage key. The one-time authorization to reset older RC payloads does not authorize resetting version-2 journey answers; all other progress must survive.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaces the questionnaire with a one-area journey and supplies a new stable store contract.

**How to apply:** Preserve version-2 J1 answers when extending the journey, never reset other stores, and keep optional named words out of completion prerequisites. The approved futures/choice ship includes completion, the Map reflection and the set-aside handoff. Consuming the one-thing answer in A4 and adding the A1 thread/bridge remain separate work.

A voluntary 90-day redo must keep prior completed words and the Map reflection available, without re-locking access earned through an earlier completed round.

**Why:** A redo is a fresh reflection, not permission to erase or invalidate an owner's earlier progress.

**How to apply:** Keep completed-round snapshots separate from the new draft. Completion side effects must be idempotent per round and preserve unrelated set-aside items.

RC journey acceptance includes 1366×680 and 1280×800 desktop plus 375×812 phone, in light and dark. Every scene must fit without internal scrolling; phones now use one scene with a sticky footer, not the old stacked questionnaire.

**Why:** The owner's review found the initial desktop layout too tall; the replacement journey brief explicitly extends the one-scene constraint to phones.

**How to apply:** Collapse optional explanations and compact spacing before reducing essential text. Do not count a signed-out screenshot or a successful build as proof of signed-in fit.

The redesigned phone start page is an exception to the room's one-screen rule: Begin must be above the fold, but its vertical five-stop journey and closing quote may continue below it.

**Why:** The updated start-page brief explicitly specifies this order, and the owner approved that interpretation before implementation.

**How to apply:** Keep room scenes within the viewport; do not truncate the phone hub's journey or shrink its full copy just to force the entire hub onto one screen.

RC review must preserve the owner's choice and completion when earlier answers are edited. Reached chapters remain available, and completed review begins at scene 1 rather than the last scene.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants to walk back through and edit all saved answers without losing the commitment already made.

**How to apply:** Track reached progress independently from the current screen and derive later reflections from current answers, not cached prose.

RC typography must share the app's A3/A4 type roles across the hub and all scenes: Tenor Sans headings, Cormorant reflective words, Questrial interface labels/buttons. Do not fix overflow by shrinking one scene's type.

**Why:** The owner explicitly prioritized consistent sizes and weights across RC.

**How to apply:** Adjust spacing and layout first; any type-size change must be shared by the whole role and listed in the delivery report.
