---
name: Phone readability
description: Phone text floors, touch areas, and fixed actions for Reality Check and A1.
---
Below 900px, functional text (instructions, helpers, body copy, button and form labels) must be at least 16px. Short status/road/stage labels and counters may remain compact, but never below 11px. Touch targets must be at least 44×44px; expand their hit areas rather than resizing their icons or text for that purpose.

**Why:** The owner approved these explicit thresholds after the phone audit; fitting a screen must not be achieved by shrinking readable text.

**How to apply:** Keep room actions and opening actions visible while their long content scrolls. Preserve copy, flow, access and saved answers. Simulated reduced-height viewport checks are not proof of native-phone keyboard behavior; state that limitation.
