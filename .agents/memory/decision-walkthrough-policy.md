---
name: Decision walkthrough boundaries
description: Approved product boundaries for the optional Own the decision experience.
---
Own the decision is standalone and optional, not an A1 ending or a prerequisite. Its answers and outcomes must never become road bricks or modify the journey answers used as context.

**Why:** The owner approved a separate decision journal, not another completion gate or a replacement for their existing mirror answers.

**How to apply:** Keep source answers read-only and preserve the words shown with each saved decision. Do not infer costs, motives, leaks or advice from prose.

“Not sure” is not a leak: show “Worth a second look” separately, below any leak box. Act and Accountability use explicit tap responses, not inferred scores. Only decision text and the final call are required; Quick has nine total screens.

**Why:** These were explicit owner decisions resolving ambiguities in the original brief.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce required dates, seven-total-screen Quick, or uncertainty classified as a leak.
