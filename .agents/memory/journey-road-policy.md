---
name: Journey road policy
description: Product constraints for road progress, completion evidence and notification pacing.
---
The road is a read-only presentation of existing progress and answers. New completion flags, saved routes, migrations, or changes to unlocks and claims require separate approval.

**Why:** The approved road build explicitly excluded new saved data. Several older exercises have no independent completion signal; movable screen pointers and prefilled answers are not reliable evidence that someone finished an exercise.

**How to apply:** Prefer explicit completion or monotonic reached flags. Otherwise use later saved work or an existing claim as conservative evidence, and disclose delayed credit rather than changing persistence to make the display exact. Optional empty work must not earn an initial brick. A prior completed Reality Check round remains earned during a redo.

Reality Check bricks advance quietly, with only a newest-brick glow; its completion gets one notification, not a notification per scene. Mirror exercises retain completion notifications. The phone summary shows six proportional stage segments; the expanded road shows every brick.

**Why:** These were explicit product adjustments to prevent interruptions inside the Reality Check and an unreadable row of bricks on phones.

**How to apply:** Do not restore per-scene Reality Check cards or a full brick row in the phone bar. Mount, hydration, refresh and Preview are not new achievements.

The road replaces the 5As spine and must retain all six navigation destinations, access rules and current-location states. Read-only applies to the progress/history display, not to the stage labels.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified that replacing the spine visually must not remove its navigation.

**How to apply:** Stage labels are accessible navigation buttons using the existing guarded router and feature gates, never gates inferred from brick counts. Other road/brick taps open history. The phone bar opens the full road; its labels navigate and its history sits below. Keep A2's Time Audit and Named Truth highlighted as A2, matching the phone mapping rather than the old desktop fallback to A1.

Do not add sequential A-stage locks or label a restriction “Opens after” a previous stage unless that prerequisite actually exists. Preserve the app's wording for real access restrictions.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved this distinction for phone navigation: the stage order is not an unlock chain, and membership/feature restrictions must not be presented as unfinished exercises.

**How to apply:** Keep the existing guarded destinations and access model when restyling navigation. Discovery tips may use browser-only storage, never new account progress fields.

Current location and earned progress are separate signals: the active stage owns “You are here,” even when later stages are already complete. A newly earned brick is a “Latest step,” not another “You.”

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested a revisit case in A2 with A3 complete; furthest completion must not misidentify where the member is working.

**How to apply:** Drive the current-stage emphasis from the active route. Keep later completion gold and the latest-brick glow independent, with no extra user-location marker.

Desktop stage labels alternate RC left, A1 right, A2 left, A3 right, A4 left, A5 right. Labels must clear the Future B star/glow and stay inside the rail without altering the road geometry.

**Why:** The owner rejected desktop placements that crowded the destination, clipped labels, or reversed stage order, then explicitly rejected shortening/narrowing the road to make room.

**How to apply:** Anchor each label beside the midpoint of its brick range. Adjust only label position, width, wrapping or distance from the road; never alter the path, height, brick spacing or destination to fit labels. Check stage order, full label bounds and clearance against the fully illuminated destination, not just an empty journey's smaller glow. Keep desktop positioning changes separate from the phone bar and sheet.
