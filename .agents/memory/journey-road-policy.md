---
name: Journey road policy
description: Product constraints for road progress, completion evidence and notification pacing.
---
The road is a read-only presentation of existing progress and answers. New completion flags, saved routes, migrations, or changes to unlocks and claims require separate approval.

**Why:** The approved road build explicitly excluded new saved data. Several older exercises have no independent completion signal; movable screen pointers and prefilled answers are not reliable evidence that someone finished an exercise.

**How to apply:** Prefer explicit completion or monotonic reached flags. Otherwise use later saved work or an existing claim as conservative evidence, and disclose delayed credit rather than changing persistence to make the display exact. Optional empty work must not earn an initial brick. A prior completed Reality Check round remains earned during a redo.

Reality Check bricks advance quietly, with only a newest-brick glow; its completion gets one notification, not a notification per scene. Mirror exercises retain completion notifications. The phone summary shows six proportional stage segments; the expanded road shows every brick.

**Why:** These were explicit product adjustments to prevent interruptions inside the Reality Check and an unreadable row of bricks on phones.

**How to apply:** Do not restore per-scene Reality Check cards or a full brick row in the phone bar. Mount, hydration, refresh and Preview are not new achievements.
