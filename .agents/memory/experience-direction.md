---
name: Experience-led presentation
description: Product direction for mirror presentation and the boundary between visual pacing and saved exercise behavior.
---
The owner wants “the entire app to feel like an experience not a workbook.” Values is the first mirror for this presentation direction; Identity, Purpose and Vision are later, separately scoped work.

**Why:** The Values experience brief explicitly establishes this product direction while restricting the first ship to visual changes.

**How to apply:** Prefer one idea at a time, the person's own saved words and calm room presentation over workbook-style lists. Do not treat a Values approval as permission to redesign other mirrors or alter saved data, steps, unlocks or claims.

Values' approved presentation is NOTICE · FLIP · NAME · ORDER. Do not use “Live it” as a Values label or restore the retired operationalize exercise. Flip is its own optional, non-blocking part; Name includes the existing meaning and evidence controls.

**Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the initial interpretation, keeping the four-part presentation aligned with the active work rather than reviving retired exercises.

**How to apply:** Keep the underlying exercise order and saved state unchanged. A claimed Values mirror must display every part as ✓ and Claimed on the opening and hub, even when subordinate saved progress flags disagree; this display rule must not repair or rewrite those flags.
