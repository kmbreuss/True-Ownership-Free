# Free course · the 36 statements (draft for Katherine)

Three areas, always in this order: Self · Time · Wealth. Three statements per pattern, per area (12 each). Only the area the person picks shows, mixed in order. The pattern tags are never shown.

## Self
**Avoiding**
- “I keep putting off the thing I need to do for me.”
- “I'm fine.”
- “I stay busy so I don't have to think about it.”
**Blaming**
- “I'd be different if the people around me were different.”
- “No one makes it easy for me.”
- “That's just how I was raised.”
**Carrying**
- “I hold everyone together, and no one holds me.”
- “I feel guilty when I put myself first.”
- “Everyone else's problems become mine.”
**Blindly hoping**
- “I'll feel better once things settle down.”
- “One day I'll get back to me.”
- “Someone will see what I need without me asking.”
## Time
**Avoiding**
- “I'll get to the important stuff when things calm down.”
- “I don't have time.”
- “I keep pushing the big things to next week.”
**Blaming**
- “If people respected my time, I'd have more of it.”
- “My calendar isn't really mine.”
- “It's the job. That's just how it is.”
**Carrying**
- “It's faster if I just do it myself.”
- “I say yes when every part of me wants to say no.”
- “If I don't do it, no one will.”
**Blindly hoping**
- “Next month will be quieter.”
- “Once this project is done, I'll slow down.”
- “Someone will notice how much I'm doing.”

## Wealth
**Avoiding**
- “I don't really look at the numbers.”
- “I'll sort the money stuff out later.”
- “I avoid the money conversation.”
**Blaming**
- “The economy is why it's tight.”
- “Prices keep going up. What can I do?”
- “If I were paid what I'm worth, I'd be fine.”
**Carrying**
- “I pay for everyone else before myself.”
- “I cover the gap so no one else has to.”
- “I can't say no when someone needs money.”
**Blindly hoping**
- “One big break will fix it.”
- “It'll work out somehow.”
- “Next year will be the year.”
