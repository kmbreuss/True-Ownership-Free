## The flow (21 screens, six movements)
The top bar shows the movement: 1 Do you own your life? · 2 What is it costing you? · 3 Look · the mirror · 4 Ask · what part do I own? · ↻ The turn · two futures · 5 Act · move toward your life.

**1 · Do you own your life?**
- **FC-01 Opening.** Video card (Katherine's short story; plays only if a video is set). H1 *Do you own your life, or does it own you?* · *When your life owns you, it doesn't happen all at once. You give it away a piece at a time.* · *Your work. Your wealth. Your time. Your family. You.* · *About 10 minutes · just you* · `I'm ready →`
- **FC-02 What owning your life means.** *Owning your life isn't about control.* · *It's knowing who you are, and aligning your days, your money, your people and your boundaries to what you want and where you're going.* Two cards: WHEN IT OWNS YOU *You react. You drift. You give it away a piece at a time.* / WHEN YOU OWN IT *You choose how this part of your life goes. Your life fits you.*
- **FC-03 Where is it owning you most?** *Three parts of your life. Pick the one that's costing you most. The others will be waiting.* Three illustrated cards, always in this order, one tap. Each shows its name, its definition, and what it covers:
  - **Self** · *The relationship you have with you.* How you speak to yourself, how you see yourself, your energy, whether you're still growing.
  - **Time** · *Who and what gets your life.* Work, family, friends, rest and play, your health.
  - **Wealth** · *Whether money works for you, or you work for it.* Cash flow, what you're building for later, feeling secure.
  - Only these three areas. Order everywhere in the app: Self, Time, Wealth. Replaces the old tie screen ("shows up equally… start somewhere else").

**2 · What is it costing you?**
- **A Tuesday** (existing screen, unchanged copy) comes before FC-04.
- **FC-04 The price.** *What is {Area} owning you costing you?* In money (a month, chips) · in hours (a week, chips) · in you (one line). Yearly totals. *This isn't blame. It's the diagnosis. And it can change.* (From the Module 1 Cost Calculator.) Replaces "What is the way your wealth runs costing you?"

**3 · Look · the mirror**
- **FC-05** *Life is a mirror. It isn't judging you. It's showing you.* H1 *And it shows you what we do instead of owning it.* Four illustrated cards with Katherine's definitions: Avoiding · Blaming · Carrying (the fixer) · Blindly hoping.
- **FC-06 Which of these have you heard yourself say?** The 12 statements for their area only (mixed order, tags hidden), tap any, at least three. Counts per pattern decide the pattern (not the area).
- **FC-07 You lean toward {Pattern}.** Big glowing illustration, definition, YOU SAID (their taps), then *None of these solve the problem. They're the cover-up. The bandaid. New shape. New story. Same thing underneath.* Tie: show both, they tap the truer one.

**4 · Ask · what part do I own?**
- **FC-08 The one thing.** *What's the one thing you've been {carrying}?* One line, required. *Don't say it out loud. Just find it.*
- **FC-09 What's underneath it?** *It's usually fear, guilt, shame or loss. Tap the one that feels true.* Fear · Guilt · Shame · Loss · Something else (opens a one-line field).
- **FC-10 It's protecting you.** Shield illustration. *{Feeling} has been protecting you.* · *Fear, guilt, shame and loss show up to keep us safe. They got you through. But they don't serve you in the long run. They keep you {carrying}.* Today: *It protects you.* Over time: *It costs you.*
- **FC-11 Look beyond it** (deep Spruce). *It's time to look beyond it.* · *Get curious.* · *Give yourself grace.* · *You did what you knew to do. Now you get to ask a braver question.*
- **FC-12 What part of this do I own?** *Not to blame. To change. Not what you caused. What you can change.* Stem: THE PART I OWN IS… (one line). *If I own everything, I own nothing.*
- **Close your eyes** (existing screen) comes next.

**↻ The turn · two futures**
- **FC-13 Future A · the same path** (grey, foggy road). *If you keep {carrying} {their one thing}…* Three stops: IN ONE MONTH · IN ONE YEAR · IN THREE YEARS. Each: one line of their own words ("What's still the same?"; one year required, others optional) and the cost worked out from FC-04 (1 month, 12 months, 36 months of money and hours). *Nothing changed. That's what it costs.*
- **FC-14 Turn around** (deep Spruce, accentuated). Faded card *← Running away from Future A* beside glowing gold card *→ Moving toward your life*. H1 *Future B isn't escaping something.* · *It's moving toward the life that fits you.*
- **FC-15 Future B · moving toward** (warm sunrise road). *If you own your part… what are you doing instead?* Same three stops, their words (one year required) and what they gain back (the same hours and money, shown as +).
- **FC-16 Picture your Tuesday.** Six steps (Morning · Work · Family · home · People · Money · How it feels), each four picture tiles plus "or in your words".
- **FC-17 Your Future B.** Their chosen tiles as one glowing scene card, their line, three feeling words, *★ {money} and {hours} back*. *This is what owning your part looks like.*
- Then the existing **note from next {month} · Choose · Willing / Not yet** screens, unchanged.
- **FC-18 Take this with you.** I'M MOVING TOWARD (their Future B line) · MY FIRST STEP THIS WEEK (one line) · *★ Step one is done.* · `Save my Future B` (the poster).

**5 · Act · move toward your life**
- **FC-19 Katherine's bridge.** *Here's what I've seen, over and over. When these five things are part of your operating system, you stop chasing your life. You align it to you. Not the other way around.* · *Most of us have one place where it slips. Let's find yours.*
- **FC-20 Five parts of your operating system.** Winding gold road to the ★, Reality Check done, A1–A5 locked with their outcomes (A1 Know who you are, so the next yes is easy · A2 See where your time and money really go · A3 Surround yourself with what lifts you · A4 Know your next move. Small, specific, yours · A5 Trust yourself again, week after week). Heading may change (Katherine deciding).
- **FC-21 What you get.** The weight comes off · You get your power back · You get a place to start · A life that fits. *Own who you are. Own what you tolerate. Own the direction you're moving.* `Join the waitlist →` then the existing waitlist ending.
